## Semnal is not an emergency service

- Semnal does not call or alert the police, an ambulance, the fire service or any other emergency service.
- The phone button on the main screen starts a call to the emergency number in your Settings. You confirm the call yourself, and your iPhone and your network make it, not Semnal. Check that the number is right for the country you're in.
- If you're in danger, call your local emergency number as soon as you can. Use Semnal as well, never instead.

## Alerts can fail or arrive late

Semnal tells the people you chose by push notification. I can't promise that an alert arrives, or arrives in time. Among other things, an alert can fail when:

- your iPhone or theirs has no internet connection. Nobody is told until the connection is back;
- their notifications are off, or they've switched your alerts off;
- their iPhone is silenced or in a Focus. Alerts arrive as normal notifications and don't sound on a silenced phone;
- your iPhone is switched off, runs out of battery, or the app is force-quit;
- Apple's or Google's services, or my server, are not working.

A person who gets your alert may not see it, and may not be able to help. Test your signals with the people you alert before you rely on them.

## What the app can't do

- Locking the iPhone or leaving the app turns off the screen light and can turn off the torch. The sound is meant to keep playing.
- iOS turns the camera off when the app isn't in front or the iPhone is locked. Video pauses then, and sound keeps recording.
- Recording stops by itself when the iPhone is almost full. The alarm goes on.
- Force-quitting the app or switching the iPhone off stops everything. No app can prevent that.
- The guards for a moved phone or an unplugged charger watch only while the guard screen stays open.
- The stop gesture is a convenience, not strong security. Anyone who watches you can learn it.

The app's Help has the full list. Read it before you need the app.

## Light and sound

- Flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy.
- The siren is loud. Don't hold the iPhone against anyone's ear while it sounds.

## Recording

Semnal can record sound and video during an alarm. The law on recording other people is different from place to place. You're responsible for following the law where you are, and for what you do with your recordings.

## The people you alert

- Add only people who agreed to be alerted by you. Both of you need Semnal.
- Anyone who has your account key can receive the alarms sent to you. Keep it like a password.
- Either of you can end the link at any time in Settings.

## Fair use
