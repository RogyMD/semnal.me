## Semnal is not an emergency service

- Semnal does not call or alert the police, an ambulance, the fire service or any other emergency service.
- The phone button on the main screen starts a call to the emergency number in your Settings. You confirm the call yourself, and your iPhone and your network make it, not Semnal. Check that the number is right for the country you're in.
- If you're in danger, call your local emergency number as soon as you can. Use Semnal as well, never instead.

## Alerts can fail or arrive late

Semnal tells the people you chose by push notification and, for a signal with SMS on, by SMS. I can't promise that an alert arrives, or arrives in time. Among other things, an alert can fail when:

- your iPhone or theirs has no internet connection. Nobody is told by notification until the connection is back;
- their notifications are off, or they've switched your alerts off;
- their iPhone is silenced. Alerts are time-sensitive notifications: they come through a Focus but don't sound on a silenced phone, unless Apple allows Semnal critical alerts and they've switched them on;
- an SMS isn't sent: Messages opens with the text, and it goes only when you press Send, or when Critical SMS sends it by itself where iOS allows that. An SMS also needs your phone network;
- your iPhone is switched off, runs out of battery, or the app is force-quit;
- Apple's or Google's services, your phone network, or my server, are not working.

A person who gets your alert may not see it, and may not be able to help. Test your signals with the people you alert before you rely on them.

## What the app can't do

- Locking the iPhone or leaving the app turns off the screen light and can turn off the torch. The sound is meant to keep playing.
- iOS turns the camera off when the app isn't in front or the iPhone is locked. Video pauses then, and sound keeps recording.
- Recording stops by itself when the iPhone is almost full. The alarm goes on.
- Force-quitting the app or switching the iPhone off stops everything. No app can prevent that.
- The guards for a moved phone or an unplugged charger watch only while the guard screen stays open.
- The stop gesture is a convenience, not strong security. Anyone who watches you can learn it.

The app's Help has the full list. Read it before you need the app.

## Light and sound

- Flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy.
- The siren is loud. Don't hold the iPhone against anyone's ear while it sounds.

## Recording

Semnal can record sound and video during an alarm, and a signal can send its recordings to the people it alerts. They can watch, save and share what they get, and a copy they keep stays with them. The law on recording other people is different from place to place. You're responsible for following the law where you are, and for what you do with your recordings.

## The people you alert

- Add only people and numbers that agreed to be alerted by you. For alerts in the app, both of you need Semnal; a number that gets an SMS doesn't.
- Anyone who has your account key can receive the alarms sent to you. Keep it like a password.
- Either of you can end the link at any time in Settings.

## Fair use

Don't use Semnal to frighten or harass anyone, to send false alarms, or to follow where someone is without their agreement. I may remove an account from the server if it's used that way.

## Your data

The [Semnal Privacy Policy](/privacy) says what leaves your iPhone and how to delete it. The server keeps alarms and their recordings for 7 days, and each account has 500 MB for recordings. **Remove All Data** in Settings deletes your account at once, and an account not used for a year is deleted with everything on the server; either way, the people you alert and who alert you are no longer linked to you.
